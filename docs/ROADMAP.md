# Dart Vader Roadmap

## Overview

Dart Vader is an open-source Discord bot designed to support Nerf and Humans versus Zombies (HvZ) communities through event management, attendance tracking, role management, and society administration tools.

The long-term goal of the project is to provide a modular, extensible, and community-driven platform that reduces administrative workload while improving the experience of players, organisers, and committee members.

This roadmap outlines the planned direction of the project. Priorities may change based on community feedback, contributor availability, and project requirements.

# Humans versus Zombies Module

**Status:** Under Consideration

Dart Vader is primarily intended as a society management platform.

At present, HvZ-specific gameplay functionality remains the responsibility of the dedicated HvZ bot, Tagger.

Future integration may be considered if there is sufficient demand and a clear benefit to combining administrative and gameplay functionality.

Potential future features include:

* [ ] Player registration
* [ ] Infection tracking
* [ ] Mission scheduling
* [ ] Killboard functionality
* [ ] Survivor statistics

No commitment is currently made regarding implementation of these features.

---

# Community Contributions

Community contributions are encouraged.

Contributors seeking work are encouraged to:

1. Review open issues.
2. Consult this roadmap.
3. Discuss major features before implementation.

Features that align with the roadmap are generally more likely to be accepted.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision for Dart Vader is to become a reliable, maintainable, and community-driven platform for Nerf and HvZ organisations.

The project aims to provide:

* Simple deployment
* Clear administration tools
* Extensible architecture
* Strong documentation
* Community-led development
* Long-term maintainability

The project will prioritise stability, security, usability, and community benefit over rapid expansion of features.
